00–04s
This little desk flower makes your AI’s activity visible.

04–09s
It follows Codex and WorkBuddy on your Mac, over USB.

09–14s
Start a task, and the flower turns. You can get on with something else.

14–24s
Different presets give it different speeds. They reflect your selected mode, not measured cloud computing power.

24–32s
When a task pauses for your answer, a breathing light lets you know.

32–37s
Answer, and the light clears as the flower starts turning again.

37–45s
If one task is still working while another waits, the flower can turn and breathe light at the same time.

45–49s
All work done, no input pending: it comes to rest.

49–56s

56–61s

61–66s
Add the electronics and follow the guide to build your flower.

66–72s
Build it, improve it, and share your flower for noncommercial use, with credit.
